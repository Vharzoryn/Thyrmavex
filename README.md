# Thyrmavex

## Beyond the Horizon

*"A dragon was never born merely to guard the mountain where it awakened."*

Azhkarion gave the dragon its first flame.

Velkaryth taught it to shed its scales and rise beyond its former self.

But eventually, even the highest mountain became too small.

The horizon called.

And the dragon answered.

Beyond the known skies lies **Thyrmavex**.

The realm of endless extension.

Here, nothing seeks to replace what already exists.

Nothing seeks to rewrite the First Flame.

Nothing interrupts the ascension carried through Velkaryth.

Instead, new paths emerge beside the old ones.

New territories appear beyond familiar borders.

New wings cross skies that once seemed unreachable.

Thyrmavex exists wherever possibility extends beyond necessity.

A hidden passage beneath an ancient fortress.

A distant kingdom beyond the sea.

A forgotten weapon buried beneath the earth.

A new form waiting to be awakened.

A story that was never required to complete the journey, yet makes the world greater simply by existing.

This is the nature of expansion.

Not replacement.

Not correction.

**Possibility.**

One flame becomes many.

One path becomes countless.

One world reveals another beyond its borders.

Yet every new branch remembers the ancient fire from which it came.

For expansion without origin is chaos.

And growth without memory eventually loses its meaning.

Thyrmavex remembers.

No matter how distant the journey becomes, invisible threads bind every new horizon to the foundations beneath Azhkarion and the transformations forged within Velkaryth.

Some dragons guard a single treasure.

Others seek every horizon.

Thyrmavex belongs to the latter.

Its boundaries cannot truly be mapped because every discovered edge creates another place beyond it.

Another path.

Another possibility.

Another sky.

And so the dragon continues forward.

Not because its world is incomplete.

But because there will always be more world to discover.

---

## The Draconic Trinity

**Azhkarion** guards the First Flame.

**Velkaryth** carries the eternal ascent.

**Thyrmavex** opens the horizons beyond.

Together, they embody the three laws of the ancient dragons:

**Origin.**

**Ascension.**

**Expansion.**

The flame is born.

The flame grows stronger.

The flame spreads beyond the horizon.

And the cycle continues for as long as dragons remain in the sky.
